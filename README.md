# Adrian Newsome

Founder of Nomad Solutions LLC, a software company in North Carolina. I design, build and run Allé, and I coach leaders and teams on agile ways of working, AI adoption, product and UX.

More than fifteen years helping organizations, leaders and teams plan and deliver together. Allé applies the same discipline to how a group plans a trip.

## Allé

Allé keeps a group trip in one place: the plan, the people, the decisions and the money, with an AI advisor that drafts itineraries, packing lists and budgets from the trip itself.

- Private beta on iOS and Android
- One codebase with Ionic, Angular and Capacitor
- Firebase and Google Cloud, with the advisor built on Gemini
- [joinalle.com](https://joinalle.com)
